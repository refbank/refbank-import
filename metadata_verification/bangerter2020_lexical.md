## Condition-level fields

Note up front: the classic-vs-new-cards manipulation (our "classic" vs "new" condition_label suffixes) is purely a stimulus-history manipulation (whether the same 8 cards recur every trial vs. new cards drawn each trial). Checking all three experiments' Method sections, this manipulation does not affect any of the 10 metadata fields audited here — group size, prior relationship, partner constancy, role constancy, population, confederates, modality, feedback, backchannel, or language are identical for "new" and "classic" within each experiment. Differences instead track experiment number (1/2 vs. 3), as reflected in our recorded rows.
